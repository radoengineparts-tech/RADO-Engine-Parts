What factors are important in connecting rod manufacturing for small engines?

Connecting rods play an important role in internal combustion engines by linking the piston to the crankshaft. In two-wheeler and three-wheeler engines, manufacturing considerations such as material selection, dimensional accuracy, surface finish, and quality inspection can influence component performance and reliability.
